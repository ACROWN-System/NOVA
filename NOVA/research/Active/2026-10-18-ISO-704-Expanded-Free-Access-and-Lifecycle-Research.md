## 1. Executive result

The expanded search did **not verify a lawful, no-cost online copy of the complete applicable ISO 704 text**. It did find a GSO-hosted public preview service and full-text-looking copies on document-sharing sites; neither can be counted as a lawful no-cost complete-text route on the evidence verified. ISO's own help page says its Online Browsing Platform makes selected sections available for free preview, including Foreword, Introduction, Scope, Normative references, and Terms & definitions. It does not represent that as the full document.

The official ISO catalogue continues to identify **ISO 704:2022, Edition 4**, as published. A separate standards-body listing surfaces a preliminary `ISO/PWI 704` record at stage `00.00`, “Proposal for new project received,” effective 2026-06-01. This is a proposal-stage lead, not a published replacement edition. The current ISO/TC 37/SC 1 public work-programme list inspected here still shows ISO 704:2022 as published and does not list a newer published ISO 704 edition.

One additional $0 route remains worth verifying by a free informational question: IIT Delhi's Central Library publicly lists subscriptions to ISO standards and says its Reader Services Division supports outside users through a library-visit facility. However, the same library's pages restrict remote electronic-resource access to authorized institute users, and they do not establish that a non-member can consult the specific ISO 704:2022 full text without charge. This route is therefore a **lead to verify**, not confirmed access.

**Decision:** AR-E02 remains **BLOCKED**. The clause/topic-to-evidence matrix remains a template only.

## 2. Official edition and lifecycle evidence

### 2.1 ISO catalogue

Official product record: https://www.iso.org/standard/79077.html

The official product page identifies ISO 704:2022 as Edition 4, published 2022-07-05, stage 60.60, with 80 pages and displayed price CHF 204. The page marks ISO 704:2009 as withdrawn and ISO 704:2022 as the current published entry in its life-cycle panel.

This verifies the catalogue's presently published edition, not any proposal's eventual outcome. It does not authorize a purchase, and the cost policy prohibits any purchase in this workstream.

### 2.2 Free preview rules

ISO help/support: https://www.iso.org/contact-iso.html

The page titled “What is the ISO Online Browsing Platform (OBP)?” states that parts of standards may be freely previewed: Foreword, Introduction, Scope, Normative references, and Terms & definitions. That is a useful legitimate route for understanding the standard's broad subject and checking title/scope metadata, but it is not a complete clause text.

### 2.3 Newer-edition lead

Separate national standards-body record: https://dps.gov.al/en/project/show/iso%3Aproj%3A94618

The Directorate of Standardization (DPS) listing describes `ISO/PWI 704`, “Terminology work — Principles and methods,” with stage `00.00` (“Proposal for new project received”), effective 2026-06-01, with ISO/TC 37/SC 1 as owner. It also identifies ISO 704:2022 as the previously published edition.

Official ISO/TC 37/SC 1 page and work programme:
- https://www.iso.org/committee/48116.html
- https://www.iso.org/committee/48116/x/catalogue/p/0/u/1/w/0/d/0

The current public committee work-programme list inspected during this search contains the published ISO 704:2022 entry and does not list a later published ISO 704 edition. The separate `PWI` record is treated as a proposal-stage lead requiring authoritative lifecycle confirmation, not as a released successor or permission to assess against a draft's unverified contents.

**Lifecycle disposition:** no newer published ISO 704 edition was verified in the inspected official sources as of 2026-10-18. Before the final baseline freeze, recheck the official product/work-programme pages and resolve the `PWI` discrepancy with an authoritative ISO committee/source if it remains.

## 3. Expanded no-cost access search

### 3.1 GSO-hosted preview service — partial preview, not complete text

A public GSO/SIMS preview endpoint was discovered at:
https://gso-sims-preview-doc-aws.s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/iso-704-2022-en.html

This is a preview-hosted resource, not an identified open-access release of the complete standard. The permissible preview scope stated in ISO's own help page remains the governing limit for this investigation. GSO's official service documentation says its system generates preview files, while GSO's official purchasing guidance says standards are distributed through its official store or member-state stores and its policy does not permit unauthorized intermediaries to publish or market standards files:
- https://gcc-gso.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SIMS/pages/844136449/ISO%2BStandards
- https://gcc-gso.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/43000771612-how-can-i-purchase-standards-files-
- https://gcc-gso.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/43000771631-is-it-possible-to-obtain-a-license-from-gso-to-publish-its-standards-files-or-market-them-

The public preview is useful for the sections intentionally made available. It does not close AR-E02.

### 3.2 Document-sharing sites and other unverified uploads

Search-engine results included full-text-looking user uploads on document-sharing platforms. The search did not establish an explicit redistribution licence, permission from ISO, or official member-body authorization for those uploads. Their apparent completeness and public accessibility are not proof of lawful distribution. They are **not accepted as sources for the controlled baseline**, and this record intentionally does not rely on or redistribute those copies.

### 3.3 Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

Official pages:
- https://www.bis.gov.in/library-services-2/?lang=en
- https://www.bis.gov.in/know-your-standard/?lang=en

BIS Library Services identifies a large standards collection including ISO publications and provides reference service to library members; its currently displayed terms list annual membership at ₹2,000 plus 18% GST. The public “Know Your Standard” portal describes search/access for Indian Standards but does not prove that the full ISO 704:2022 text or an identical adopted edition is available free of charge. This fails the current $0-only constraint as a membership route.

### 3.4 IIT Delhi Central Library — possible in-person lead, not yet verified

Official library pages:
- Standards and access guide: https://library.iitd.ac.in/page/user-guides/
- All resources: https://library.iitd.ac.in/page/allresources/
- Reader Services Division / visiting users: https://library.iitd.ac.in/divisions/rsd/
- Electronic Resources Division: https://library.iitd.ac.in/divisions/erd/

The Central Library identifies “ISO Standards” among its subscribed standards resources. The Reader Services Division says it supports outside users through a library-visit facility. The library's remote e-resource access is restricted to authorized institute users; its published pages do not say whether a non-member can consult this specific standard at the library without charge.

**Unresolved lawful $0 inquiry:** Ask the Reader Services Division and Electronic Resources Division whether (a) an outside reader can consult the licensed ISO 704:2022 full text onsite under the visit facility, without a fee, or (b) the subscription is restricted to authorized users and the specific publication cannot be viewed by visitors. This is an informational question only; do not book paid membership, request a paid service or proceed with any fee. Contacts published by IIT Delhi are `rsd@library.iitd.ac.in`, `erd@library.iitd.ac.in`, and phone `+91-11-2659-7017` / `+91-11-2659-6627/6631`.

### 3.5 Other institutional subscription routes

IIT Delhi is one verified example of a library that subscribes to ISO standards. Other universities, research institutions or employers may also hold licensed access. A public search cannot establish the entitlement of a person who is not an authorized member of a subscribing institution. Access through another institution counts only if the library confirms that (1) the exact applicable text is available, (2) the user is authorized to consult it, and (3) the route entails no cost or payment obligation.
