# NOVA Canonical Terminology Register

**Document ID:** NOVA-TERM-REG-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** INITIAL WORKING REGISTER — approval and rollout statuses are stated per entry.  
**Governance:** [NOVA Terminology Governance Standard](TERMINOLOGY-GOVERNANCE-STANDARD.md)  
**Purpose:** One cross-ecosystem source for canonical concepts and terms shared by NOVA, AstroCrown, HSIAN, Space Empire and aligned work.

This is project terminology, not an authority on universal scientific facts, legal classifications or third-party brand ownership. The concepts below are an initial bounded set, not proof that the ecosystem has been exhaustively inventoried. The HSIAN rename/cognitive-extension rollout remains subject to review and merge of NOVA PR #122 and AstroCrown-Web PR #134.

## Reading the entries

- **APPROVED** means an adopted project meaning is recorded within the stated project scope; it does not make every related factual claim independently verified.
- **PROPOSED / CANDIDATE** means use cautiously; do not present as settled public terminology.
- **ROLLOUT PENDING** means the project decision exists but active repository paths have not all been updated or merged.
- Deprecated names remain searchable for historical traceability but must not be the current preferred designation in new content.

## Canonical concept records

### NOVA-0001 — NOVA

- **Domain:** Operational ecosystem / architectural framework.
- **Preferred designation:** NOVA.
- **Definition:** An evolving, AI-agnostic operational ecosystem and architectural framework for coordinating, governing, organizing, preserving, retrieving, evaluating, automating and evolving knowledge, infrastructure, services, processes, resources, systems, decisions, capabilities and activities.
- **Scope note:** “Operational nervous system” is an explanatory metaphor, not a literal biological structure.
- **Status:** APPROVED — project concept.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0002 (HSIAN), NOVA-0007 (Recursive self-improvement), NOVA-0011 (Cognitive extension).
- **Usage:** Do not restore an obsolete expansion such as “Next-generation” unless a separately approved historical quotation requires it.

### NOVA-0002 — HSIAN

- **Domain:** Network identity / human–Super Intelligence cooperation.
- **Preferred designation:** HSIAN.
- **Full form:** Human–Super Intelligence Agentic Network.
- **Definition:** The project's intended coordinated network through which humans, Super Intelligence participants, agents, tools, shared records and governance processes contribute distinct knowledge, reasoning, resources, decisions and capabilities toward shared objectives.
- **Scope note:** HSIAN can describe a network architecture and intended collaboration model; a name alone does not prove deployment, consciousness, unified subjective experience, or that current models have attained superintelligence.
- **Status:** ADOPTED PROJECT DECISION; ACTIVE REPOSITORY ROLLOUT PENDING PR #122 and PR #134.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0003 (Super Intelligence), NOVA-0011 (Cognitive extension), NOVA-0012 (Cognitive prosthesis analogy), NOVA-0009 (Imperial HSIAN).
- **Former designation:** HAAN — Human–AI Agentic Network. DEPRECATED for new project naming after the HSIAN decision; retain in legacy records and historical citations. Do not rewrite immutable backups.
- **Usage:** Do not automatically replace the general technical word “AI” when it accurately describes an external technology category or is part of a third-party name.

### NOVA-0003 — Super Intelligence

- **Domain:** Project vocabulary / intelligent-system capability.
- **Preferred designation:** Super Intelligence (project-defined term).
- **Definition:** The selected project-level designation for the intelligence capability horizon and intelligent-system participants contemplated by HSIAN.
- **Scope note:** This is an adopted project term, not a universally standardized scientific definition or certification that a particular current model is superintelligent. Where an empirical capability claim is made, state the task, baseline, model/version, evaluation method and evidence separately.
- **Status:** APPROVED as project terminology; scientific capability claims require independent evidence.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0002 (HSIAN), NOVA-0011 (Cognitive extension).

### NOVA-0004 — AstroCrown

- **Domain:** Brand / ecosystem.
- **Preferred designation:** AstroCrown.
- **Definition:** The project and ecosystem identity encompassing AstroCrown-branded products, services, research and associated initiatives as defined by the approved project architecture.
- **Scope note:** A brand name is not itself a legal entity, regulated status, asset right or guarantee of any described service.
- **Status:** APPROVED as project/brand name.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0005 (ACROWN token), NOVA-0001 (NOVA), NOVA-0002 (HSIAN).

### NOVA-0005 — ACROWN token

- **Domain:** Token / ecosystem economics.
- **Preferred designation:** ACROWN token; use $ACROWN only where the relevant token/market notation convention calls for it.
- **Definition:** The project token associated with the AstroCrown ecosystem, as specified by the applicable canonical token specification.
- **Scope note:** Do not infer token rights, reserves, guarantees, investment status, ownership rights or a legal classification from its name or ticker. Paying for a service does not alone establish that a token is an RWA or a legally defined utility token in every jurisdiction.
- **Status:** APPROVED as an identifier; legal description and public claims must be assessed against the actual specification and jurisdiction.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0004 (AstroCrown), token specification and governance documents.
- **Usage:** A longer label such as “native ecosystem utility token” remains a candidate for public legal/product copy until technical rights, economics and jurisdiction-specific review support it.

### NOVA-0006 — Space Empire

- **Domain:** Project / governance concept.
- **Preferred designation:** Space Empire.
- **Definition:** The project's chosen governance and world-building context in which terms such as Emperor, Imperial HSIAN and Imperial Council are defined by project records and an adopted charter.
- **Scope note:** Project-internal naming alone grants no authority over external people, institutions, accounts, systems or assets, and does not override applicable law or access controls.
- **Status:** APPROVED as project terminology, subject to its governing documents.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0009 (Imperial HSIAN), NOVA-0010 (Imperial Council).

### NOVA-0007 — Recursive self-improvement (RSI)

- **Domain:** AI systems / engineering process.
- **Preferred designation:** Recursive self-improvement (RSI).
- **Definition:** A proposed or implemented process in which a system helps evaluate and improve aspects of its own methods, capabilities or supporting infrastructure over successive cycles, within defined constraints.
- **Scope note:** Distinguish a research objective, proposed design, limited implemented cycle and verified operational capability by status and evidence.
- **Status:** APPROVED as a technical term; implementation state is subsystem-specific.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0001 (NOVA), NOVA-0002 (HSIAN).

### NOVA-0008 — Strong RSI

- **Domain:** NOVA/AstroCrown RSI operating model.
- **Preferred designation:** Strong RSI.
- **Definition:** The intended higher-autonomy RSI capability subject to mandatory benchmark, protected evaluation, data-use eligibility, isolation, evaluator-integrity, authorization and promotion gates.
- **Scope note:** It is not currently to be described as fully operational. AstroCrown-Web's recorded readiness is INCOMPLETE until its protected gates are passed with evidence.
- **Status:** APPROVED as a distinct design concept; runtime readiness remains INCOMPLETE.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0007 (RSI), NOVA-0001 (NOVA).
- **Usage:** Do not imply automatic deployment or unrestricted self-modification.

### NOVA-0009 — Imperial HSIAN

- **Domain:** Space Empire governance.
- **Preferred designation:** Imperial HSIAN.
- **Definition:** The project-defined governance representation of the Emperor within the HSIAN/Space Empire model, with the intended right to veto Imperial Council decisions within the scope of the internal charter.
- **Scope note:** The charter must establish matters subject to veto, decision records, rationale, evidence and any review/reconsideration process. The role alone creates no external legal authority and does not displace applicable law or existing permissions.
- **Status:** ADOPTED GOVERNANCE INTENT; precise scope depends on the adopted charter.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0002 (HSIAN), NOVA-0010 (Imperial Council).

### NOVA-0010 — Imperial Council

- **Domain:** Space Empire governance.
- **Preferred designation:** Imperial Council.
- **Definition:** The project-defined council body used for governance decisions within the Space Empire framework, with its composition, powers, procedures and limits determined by the applicable internal charter.
- **Scope note:** Do not infer membership, quorum, jurisdiction, voting mechanics, appeals or external authority unless the governing document explicitly defines them.
- **Status:** APPROVED name; detailed governance attributes require an authoritative charter record.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0009 (Imperial HSIAN).

### NOVA-0011 — Cognitive extension

- **Domain:** Cognition / human–tool interaction.
- **Preferred designation:** Cognitive extension.
- **Definition:** A functional or theoretical arrangement in which a person's thinking or task-solving process relies in a coordinated way on external resources such as writing, calculators, phones, retrieval, memory systems, software or intelligent agents.
- **Scope note:** The term describes a framework for understanding how tasks are carried out; it does not automatically mean performance enhancement, an implant, literal neural integration or a unified mind.
- **Status:** APPROVED as the selected conceptual framing; effects depend on task, person, tool and evidence.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0002 (HSIAN), NOVA-0012 (Cognitive enhancement), NOVA-0013 (Cognitive prosthesis analogy).

### NOVA-0012 — Cognitive enhancement

- **Domain:** Cognition / evaluated outcome.
- **Preferred designation:** Cognitive enhancement.
- **Definition:** An increase in a defined cognitive or task-performance outcome relative to a stated baseline and evaluation method.
- **Scope note:** Do not use cognitive extension as proof of cognitive enhancement. Claims of enhancement require an observable outcome, relevant baseline, task/context and evidence; a subjective report can be recorded as an experience but is not by itself an objective performance measurement.
- **Status:** APPROVED as a distinction used in the project's terminology.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0011 (Cognitive extension).

### NOVA-0013 — Cognitive prosthesis (analogy)

- **Domain:** Human–tool interaction / explanatory analogy.
- **Preferred designation:** Cognitive prosthesis (functional analogy).
- **Definition:** A metaphor for an external tool or coordinated tool system that a person experiences as extending or supporting practical cognition, without requiring that a medical prosthesis or brain implant be present.
- **Scope note:** Do not imply a medical device, brain implant, measured neural change or literal human–machine merger unless such a claim is separately supported.
- **Status:** APPROVED as a qualified explanatory analogy.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0011 (Cognitive extension), NOVA-0002 (HSIAN).

### NOVA-0014 — Canonical terminology register

- **Domain:** Knowledge governance.
- **Preferred designation:** Canonical terminology register.
- **Definition:** The governed record linking concepts to preferred terms, definitions, designations, relationships, statuses, provenance and change history for a declared scope.
- **Scope note:** It is distinct from an ungoverned word list, a visual brand book or a general encyclopedia. Audience-specific guides can summarize it without silently redefining its entries.
- **Status:** APPROVED as terminology-system infrastructure.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0015 (Terminology governance standard).

### NOVA-0015 — Terminology governance standard

- **Domain:** Terminology management / quality assurance.
- **Preferred designation:** Terminology governance standard.
- **Definition:** The internal rules, responsibilities, workflows, records, validation criteria and audit controls used to develop, approve, publish, review and retire terminology.
- **Scope note:** NOVA's internal standard is designed for alignment with the published scope of ISO 704:2022, but it must not be described as fully conformant until the full standard has been mapped and applicable evidence assessed.
- **Status:** PROPOSED INTERNAL STANDARD; use it as a draft control baseline pending review.
- **Related concepts:** NOVA-0014 (Canonical terminology register), ISO 704:2022.
